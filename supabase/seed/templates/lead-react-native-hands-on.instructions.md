## About this assessment

This is a **hands-on debugging assessment** for the **Lead React Native Developer** role. It has **4 questions** and lasts **150 minutes** in one sitting. The timer starts when you press **Start assessment** and keeps running even if you close the browser.

Each question gives you a small codebase and a list of bug reports. Find the root causes and fix the code directly in the editor. We assess the correctness of your fixes and how well they address the root cause, so a short comment next to a non-obvious fix helps the reviewers.

- Every question comes with read-only tests that describe the expected behaviour. Press **Run tests** as often as you like; nothing needs to be installed. The editor also underlines errors.
- **Question 3** (Spring Boot): Java can't run in the browser, so its tests check your code's structure. Reviewers also run the JUnit tests on your code afterwards.

## How to work

- Open the list of questions from the bar on the far left (hover or click the list icon). Use **Mark for review** to come back to a question. The panel button next to it hides the problem statement to give the code more room.
- The **coding assistant** (the sparkle button) gives short hints. It won't write the solution for you, it answers a limited number of questions, and reviewers can see what you asked.
- Your work is saved automatically every few seconds on this device and on Zycus servers. If your connection drops, keep working: your changes sync when it returns.
- If the page reloads or your browser closes, sign in again with the same email and access code. You'll continue where you left off, with the time that remains.
- Press **Submit** when you're done. You can't change your answers after submitting. When the time is up, your work is submitted automatically.

## Rules

- Work alone. Don't use other websites, other AI tools, notes or other people. The built-in coding assistant is the only help allowed.
- Stay in full-screen mode, in this tab, on one screen, with your webcam on and your face visible.
- Pasting, copying the questions, right-clicking and developer tools are disabled. Attempts are logged.
- Don't share these questions or your answers with anyone, during or after the assessment.
